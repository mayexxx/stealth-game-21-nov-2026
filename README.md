The Quiet Gallery — a top-down stealth game. A museum after dark: lift three relics from each of three galleries, then reach the east door unseen. Guards walk their rounds with vision cones that stop at the walls; dark floor hides you, crates block sight, and a thrown pebble pulls a guard away to investigate. Get spotted and the chase is on — break line of sight and they search, then give up. Clear all three galleries without ever being seen for a ghost run.

## Run it

```sh
node serve.ts
```

Then open http://localhost:8080 — WASD or arrows to move, hold Shift to sneak, Q or click to throw a pebble. No build step, no network requests: the game is plain static files under `game/`, and all sound is synthesised with WebAudio.
